About Priority Limousine Collections

Founded in 2015, Priority Limousine Collections emerged from a vision to transform luxury transportation in Singapore. What began as a boutique service with three premium vehicles has grown into the city-state's most prestigious fleet of luxury automobiles, serving discerning clients who expect nothing but excellence.

Our mission is to deliver unparalleled luxury transportation experiences through a perfect blend of premium vehicles, professional service, and innovative technology. We uphold the values of discretion, punctuality, and personalized attention, ensuring every journey with us is memorable for all the right reasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We provide comprehensive coverage throughout Singapore and select cross-border services to Malaysia. Our service area includes all major hotels, airports, and business districts.

How do I book a service?

Booking is available 24/7 through our mobile app, website, or by calling our concierge team. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for guaranteed availability.

What types of vehicles are available?

Our fleet includes Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Rolls-Royce, and other luxury vehicles. All vehicles are latest models and maintained to the highest standards.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, digital payments (PayNow, GrabPay), and corporate billing arrangements. Payment is required at the time of booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before the scheduled service. Late cancellations may incur a 50% charge. No-shows are charged in full.
